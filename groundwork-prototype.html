Funding

Government funding you can actually get

Most owners never apply for government funding, and it is almost never because they were turned down. It is because nobody told them which programmes exist. Groundwork checks your profile against every federal, Ontario and City of Toronto programme we track, and says which ones you can get.

We also tell you what is closed to you, and why. Futurpreneur stops at 39. Starter Company Plus needs a registered Toronto business that is already trading. Listing a programme you cannot get wastes your afternoon.

Every programme shows where the information came from and the day we last checked it. This tells you what to apply for. It is not an approval.

No revenue forecast, by design

Groundwork has no location performance data, so it does not predict your sales. The plan gives you the revenue structure a lender expects and does the arithmetic on assumptions you supply, marked as yours throughout the document.

Common questions

Is this a loan pre-approval?
No, and nothing in it should be shown to anyone as one. Groundwork tells you which programmes your profile fits and where to apply. The lender decides.
Do I need an account before I see anything?
Yes. The profile drives every stage — your age band, residency, business stage and capital all change what you are shown. You can build the profile and browse three neighbourhoods before paying.
Toronto only?
For now, yes. The federal and Ontario financing layers already apply province-wide, but the map and the cost benchmarks are Toronto.
How current is the data?
Census attributes are 2021 — the most recent published, and the same vintage every competitor works from. Financing records carry the date each was last checked, shown in the product.
What if I am not eligible for anything?
You will be told that plainly, with the reason. That is a real outcome and worth $99.99 to learn in an evening rather than over three months of applications.
Can I change my numbers after buying?
Yes. Thirty days to revise any assumption and re-export. Override any cost line with a real quote and it is marked as yours in the document.
